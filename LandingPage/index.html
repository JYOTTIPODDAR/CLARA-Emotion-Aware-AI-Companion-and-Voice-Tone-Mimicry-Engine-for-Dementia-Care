ABOUT CLARA

Technology that adapts to the patient.

Clara is designed to provide compassionate, personalized conversational support for people living with dementia and Alzheimer's disease.

Instead of expecting patients to learn a complex application, Clara provides a simple voice-first experience while caregivers remain in control.

🧠

Built Around the Patient

Patient context, familiar memories, communication preferences and calming responses can be configured by caregivers.
